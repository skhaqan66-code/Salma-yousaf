About

Content built on research, not guesswork

I'm an SEO content writer who helps businesses in the UK, USA and Canada turn search intent into clear, useful content. My work spans blog posts, product descriptions, landing pages, service pages and collection pages — all built around what readers are actually looking for, not just what a keyword tool says.

Over several years of remote work, I've learned that good SEO content starts with research: understanding a business, its audience, and how people actually search for it. From there, I apply keyword research, search intent analysis, on-page SEO and E-E-A-T principles, along with newer practices like AEO, GEO and SXO, to write content that performs for both search engines and the people reading it.

I care about content that's easy to scan, genuinely useful, and structured well enough that both a reader and a search engine can find what they need quickly.

Based in Punjab, Pakistan — working remotely with clients worldwide.
